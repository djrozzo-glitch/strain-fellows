# Higher & Higher roadmap

## Next release

- Validate zone pacing and readability during full runs, including transitions and gameplay at peak difficulty.
- Check touch, overscroll, safe areas, resume and rotation on physical iOS and Android devices.
- Profile asset decoding and frame pacing on lower-end phones. Optimize or defer noncritical assets where measurements justify it.
- Tune platform spacing and pickup frequency through playtesting. Check that every generated route remains reachable as difficulty increases.

## Arcade polish

- Refine landing, spring and rocket feedback using the existing illustrated effects.
- Review accessibility, including keyboard focus, reduced-motion preferences, sound controls and score readability.
- Verify personal-best persistence and score sharing across target browsers, including the clipboard fallback.

## Global leaderboard

- Design score submission, validation, player identity and abuse controls.
- Add a score service and integrate a compact leaderboard into the existing result flow.
- Test failures and offline play while retaining the local personal best.
